Market Day Help

Market Day by Capable Clover

How do I connect Square?

Tap your picture at the top right of any tab to open Settings, then tap Connect Square and sign in on Square's page. Your sales come straight to your device.

Where do my markets come from?

Market Day reads market days from your calendar. Turn on Calendar in Settings. You can also add a market yourself from the Markets tab.

Why don't my miles look right?

Miles come from addresses. Open Settings › Markets, Costs and Miles and check your home address and each market's address. Miles update on their own after that.

I take cash. How do I count it?

After a market, Home asks if you had any cash. You can also add it on that market's page. If you ring up cash in Square, it's already counted.

Where is my data?

Only on your device and in your own iCloud backup. We don't have servers or accounts, so we can't see it. Read the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

Settings › Erase All My Data removes everything from the app. This can't be undone.
