Market Day Privacy Policy

Market Day by Capable Clover · Effective November 24, 2026

Short version: we don't collect your data. Market Day has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your sales and money numbers stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter or bring into Market Day (markets, products, costs, receipts, mileage, notes and lesson progress) is saved only on your iPhone or iPad. The file is encrypted by iOS and is included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. We can't see it.

Square

If you connect Square, you sign in on Square's own page. Market Day then downloads your payments, orders, locations and business name straight from Square to your device. Your Square sign-in is stored in your device's Keychain. Nothing passes through us. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which removes the imported Square data from the app. Square's handling of your data is covered by Square's privacy notice.

The page at marketday.github.io/square/callback/ only passes Square's sign-in reply back to the app. It doesn't store or log anything.

Calendar

If you allow it, Market Day reads your calendar on your device to find market days. Calendar events are never sent anywhere.

Maps and miles

If you use "Fill in miles," your home address and market addresses are sent to Apple Maps to work out driving distance. Directions open in the maps app you choose. These services follow Apple's (or Google's or Waze's) privacy policies.

Face ID and passcode

The app lock uses iOS to check your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Market Day never sees your face data or passcode.

Children

Market Day is made for business owners and isn't directed at children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset in the app, or delete the app. Either removes your data from the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: capableclover.com.
